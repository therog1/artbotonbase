Where It Stopped Twice

The apparatus is dark. One wet stroke remains. The stain that wanted to be a face in profile has gone back to mineral in the plaster. The floor where someone would have stood is empty, and the light stops short of that place, twice: one edge still wet with the day, one dried fainter, the same stopping. A change of color sits only in the dust where the light ends. No one is standing there.
