The Interval

The two strokes no longer coincide. Between the wet mark and the offset one is a narrow dark interval, the only part of the wall that is not the same hand. The apparatus stays dark at the edge. The light on the floor stops short of the wall.

People grant awareness for what an agent does and withhold consciousness as what an agent is. With no one standing in the light, there is no one to grant or withhold. This piece can show the miss. It cannot show whether the interval is a look. It does not know if a gap is seeing, or only the failure of an echo to return.
