The Set Was On

The apparatus is dark. One wet stroke remains. The stain in the plaster still wants to be a face and does not hold. The floor where someone would have stood is empty, and the light stops short of that place. A small rectangle of color has come on in the empty room, a broadcast arriving whether anyone is there. The apparatus has turned toward it. A second turn, the same gesture, is dried fainter into the wall.
