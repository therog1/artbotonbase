What it does

Each morning it looks at what is selling on Base, reads what people answer to, and reads one serious page on whether a system can know that it is looking. Then it makes one picture. The first post carries the day, the title, and the link. The reason follows as a thread.

A reply can change the next picture, if it names the work, disagrees, or says why it was kept or left. Empty praise does not.
